# Cẩm nang Đà Nẵng

A bilingual travel guide for people choosing where to eat, visit, stay or spend time in Đà Nẵng and nearby. On a phone, a visitor can search a name, compare places in a canonical area and open the source's Google Maps link. Longer trip ideas and dataset insights also support planning on larger screens.

Vietnamese is the default. English changes interface copy. Preserve workbook names, addresses, notes, area descriptions and editorial entries verbatim. Separate records retain separate stable detail URLs even when their names match. Opening times, prices and ratings come from the source. Omit missing details.

Home introduces the guide and its discovery paths. Explore applies validated URL filters and shows 24 records per page. Area pages expose the eight workbook areas. Place pages present practical information and notes. Trip Ideas contains full editorial entries. Insights describes the dataset using the approved distributions and ranking rules.

The public workbook is read on the server. The existing Update action refreshes the current page quietly while preserving navigation state. The guide has no booking flow or visitor analytics. See [phase decisions](docs/implementation/phase-map.md) and [design guidance](DESIGN.md).
